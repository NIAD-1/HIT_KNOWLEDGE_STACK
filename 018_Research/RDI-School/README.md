# School of Research, Development & Innovation (RDI)

> Mission: Learn how to systematically discover new knowledge, invent new
> technologies, validate ideas, protect intellectual property, and transform
> research into commercially successful products.

20 modules across five arcs: Research Foundations (1-6), Innovation & IP (7-9),
R&D Strategy (10-14), Advanced Practice (15-19), and the Capstone (20).

Each topic inside a module has its own folder with Research, Assignment,
and Learning notes — same pattern as the rest of the HIT_Knowledge_Stack vault.
