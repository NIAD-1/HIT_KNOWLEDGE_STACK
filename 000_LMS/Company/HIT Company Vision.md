# HIT Company Vision

HIT is an industrial intelligence company building IIoT devices, platforms, and intelligence systems for regulated and operationally complex industries.

## Target Domains

- Pharma
- Supply chain
- Food chain
- Food manufacturing
- Healthcare
- Industrial operations
- Regulated infrastructure

## Product Lines

| Product Line | What It Builds | Mission Sources |

| HIT Industrial Edge Devices | Sensor nodes, gateways, secure firmware, industrial protocol bridges | [[000_LMS/Missions/Industrial Edge Gateway Mission]] |
| HIT Food Safety Monitoring | Monitoring devices and data systems for food environments | [[000_LMS/Missions/Food Safety Monitoring Device Mission]] |

## Company Rule

Every school should connect to a capability.

Every capability should connect to a mission.

Every mission should produce evidence.

Every evidence trail should move HIT closer to a real product, customer, or deployment.
