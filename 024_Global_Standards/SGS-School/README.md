# School of Global Standards (SGS)

> Mission: To forge an unassailable bridge between cutting-edge Industrial
> IoT (IIoT) engineering and uncompromising international health regulations.
> Transforms global standards into an automated, defensible competitive advantage.

15 modules organized in four arcs: Enterprise Quality & IIoT Governance (1-4),
Industrial Data Integrity & Edge Security (5-8), Cold-Chain Logistics &
Sensor Metrology (9-12), and Continuous Audit Readiness (13-15).

Each topic inside a module has its own folder with Research, Assignment,
and Learning notes — maintaining the HIT_Knowledge_Stack pattern.
