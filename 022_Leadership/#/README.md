# School of Leadership & Organizational Excellence (LOE)

> Mission: Learn to lead yourself, lead other people, and design the
> organization that carries a funded, engineered product to scale —
> hiring, culture, management, governance, and executive leadership.

20 modules across five arcs: Leadership Foundations (1-4), People & Team
Building (5-9), Organizational Design & Culture (10-13), Executive &
Strategic Leadership (14-19), and the Capstone (20).

Each topic inside a module has its own folder with Research, Assignment,
and Learning notes — same pattern as the rest of the HIT_Knowledge_Stack vault.
