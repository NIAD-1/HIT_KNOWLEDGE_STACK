# School of Entrepreneurship & Venture Building (EVB)

> Mission: Learn to build, operate, and scale the venture as a whole —
> pulling research, product, finance, and leadership into one operating
> discipline for taking a company from zero to a durable, scaling business.

20 modules across five arcs: Entrepreneurial Foundations (1-4), Venture
Building Mechanics (5-9), Growth & Scaling (10-14), Advanced Venture
Strategy (15-19), and the Capstone (20).

Each topic inside a module has its own folder with Research, Assignment,
and Learning notes — same pattern as the rest of the HIT_Knowledge_Stack vault.
