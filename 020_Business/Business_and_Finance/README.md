# School of Business Strategy & Corporate Finance (BSCF)

> Mission: Learn to build, finance, and strategically steer a company
> around a validated, engineered product — from unit economics and
> financial modeling through fundraising, valuation, and long-term
> corporate strategy.

20 modules across five arcs: Strategy Foundations (1-4), Finance &
Accounting (5-9), Fundraising & Capital (10-13), Corporate Strategy &
Growth (14-19), and the Capstone (20).

Each topic inside a module has its own folder with Research, Assignment,
and Learning notes — same pattern as the rest of the HIT_Knowledge_Stack vault.
