# School of Industrial Domains (ID)

> Mission: Learn the operational realities, buyer behavior, technical
> requirements, and market dynamics of every industry HIT's Industrial
> Monitoring Platform could serve.

20 modules across five arcs: Cross-Domain Foundations (1-3), Core Domains
(4-12), Domain Deep Dives (13-16), Vertical Strategy (17-19), and the
Capstone (20).

Each topic inside a module has its own folder with Research, Assignment,
and Learning notes — same pattern as the rest of the HIT_Knowledge_Stack vault.
